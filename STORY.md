# VANGUARD: BLACKOUT PROTOCOL — Story Bible

> A story-based third-person shooter built on the **Vanguard Operator** rig.
> The old practice range is now **Mission 0 — Calibration**. Every mission after it
> takes place in a **completely new world**: different location, different time of day,
> different enemy faction colour, different architecture.

---

## 1. The World

**Year: 2029. City: Zlatograd** — a fictional Adriatic port city in the Balkans
("gold city"). Once a shipbuilding and fishing town, Zlatograd was shattered by
conflict in the 1990s and never fully rebuilt.

Three years ago the government signed a **"Reconstruction Mandate"** with
**Meridian Global Security**, a private military company. Meridian promised roads,
power and jobs. What they actually built was **Project BLACKOUT**:

- **The Lattice** — a city-wide grid of signal relays, cameras and jammers that
  listens to every phone, every radio, every camera in Zlatograd.
- **Autonomous weapons** — Meridian is testing drone-linked infantry weapons on
  the population. The docks receive the shipments. The construction sites hide
  the relays. The villages pay the price.
- **The Blackout** — once a month, Meridian cuts power to whole districts
  "for maintenance". Nobody is allowed to ask why.

Meridian's official story: *stability through control.*
The truth, which only a handful of people know: **Viktor Sorin is selling the
Lattice to three governments** — and the first live test is scheduled in 72 hours.

## 2. Characters

### Captain Elias "Vanguard" Voss — THE PLAYER (you)
- **Age:** 38. **Role:** Protagonist. Ex-special forces, Task Force Kestrel.
- **Backstory:** Three years ago Voss was ordered to clear a Zlatograd
  neighbourhood that turned out to hold only civilians. He refused the order,
  was discharged in disgrace, and has been working private contracts ever since.
  His old callsign — **VANGUARD** — stuck. The tan armour you see in the game
  is his.
- **Personality:** Quiet, dry, loyal. Does the job, asks few questions — until
  the questions matter. He came back to Zlatograd for one last contract.
  He stayed for the people.
- **Arc:** From mercenary-for-hire → the man who breaks the Lattice and walks
  away clean. He never wanted to be a hero. He just couldn't watch it happen again.

### Mara Kovač — HANDLER / INTEL (radio voice)
- **Age:** 34. **Role:** Your handler, fixer and conscience. Ex-signals officer.
- **Backstory:** Grew up in Zlatograd. Her younger brother **Tomas** was a
  Meridian informant who fed her the Lattice schematics — and was found dead
  after Mission 1's village was raided. Mara has been hunting the truth ever
  since. She recruited Voss because he is the only operator she trusts.
- **Personality:** Sharp, sarcastic, warm underneath. She jokes when she's scared.
- **Arc:** She starts the campaign wanting revenge. She ends it wanting the
  city back. Her brother's data cache (Mission 3) is the key to everything.

### Sergeant Dario "Brick" Horvat — ALLY (in person from Mission 5)
- **Age:** 41. **Role:** Heavy weapons / engineer. Voss's old squadmate.
- **Backstory:** The only man who stood next to Voss on the day he refused the
  order. Brick got out too. Now he runs a garage in the old town and fixes
  anything with an engine — including, in Mission 5, a stolen Meridian APC.
- **Personality:** Loud, funny, unkillable. Calls everyone "kid".
- **Gameplay:** In Mission 5 Brick fights beside you as an **AI ally** (green
  tint) — he follows, covers you, and shoots Meridian guards.

### Viktor Sorin — ANTAGONIST
- **Age:** 58. **Role:** CEO of Meridian Global Security. Ex-colonel.
- **Backstory:** Sorin believes cities are machines and people are parts.
  He built the Lattice to prove it. In 72 hours he will demonstrate a live
  city-wide blackout to his buyers — with Zlatograd's people as the test.
- **Personality:** Calm, courteous, terrifying. He offers Voss a fortune to
  walk away. Twice. Voss refuses twice.
- **Gameplay:** Final boss in Mission 5. Black-red armour, gold visor,
  **300 HP**, elite guards. Killing him triggers the ending.

### Dr. Lena Aris — DEFECTOR (Mission 2)
- **Age:** 29. **Role:** Meridian systems engineer. Built the Lattice relays.
- **Backstory:** She joined Meridian to build communications for hospitals.
  She stayed to build surveillance for Sorin. When she saw the weapons
  manifests, she copied the master codes and ran — to the construction site
  where her relay crew was based. She is hiding in the site office.
- **Gameplay:** **Rescue target** in Mission 2. After you clear her guards she
  follows you to extraction and hands Mara the master codes (story key).

### Old Petar Jovanović — INFORMANT (Mission 1)
- **Age:** 71. **Role:** Village elder of Gornja Luka.
- **Backstory:** Petar's village was the first Lattice test site. He hid a
  Meridian patrol's radio codes in his church. Meridian came back for them.
- **Gameplay:** **Rescue target** in Mission 1. He gives you the patrol
  frequencies — which is why Mara can hear Meridian's chatter for the rest
  of the campaign.

### Commander Ilya Ren — MID-BOSS (Mission 4)
- **Age:** 47. **Role:** Meridian field commander, docks.
- **Backstory:** Sorin's right hand. Ran the village raid. Personally
  responsible for Tomas Kovač's death — Mara doesn't know that yet.
  She finds out in Mission 4. It changes her.
- **Gameplay:** Boss in Mission 4 (**200 HP**, dark navy/black). Killing him
  unlocks the weapons manifests that prove the live test.

### Nadia Petrović — CONTACT (Mission 4, radio)
- **Age:** 26. **Role:** Dock crane operator / smuggler.
- **Backstory:** Nadia's family lost their fishing boats to Meridian's port
  monopoly. She now smuggles people and medicine through the docks at night.
  She guides you through the container maze in Mission 4.

### Tomas Kovač — (mentioned, deceased)
- Mara's brother. Informant. His data cache on the downtown relay (Mission 3)
  contains the Lattice kill-switch. Everything Mara does, she does for him.

## 3. Enemy Factions (same soldier model, different colour)

Every enemy is the **same Vanguard soldier rig** — re-tinted per faction so you
always know who you're fighting. Visor colour = faction accent.

| Faction | Missions | Body tint | Visor | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Village Militia** | M1 | Olive / khaki `#6b7a4a` | Amber | Patrols, slow, inaccurate. They don't want to be there either. |
| **Meridian Contractors** | M2 | Charcoal `#4a4d52` | Red | Guards + patrols. Professional. |
| **Meridian Elite** | M3 | Black `#2a2c30` | Red | Snipers hold rooftops, patrols hold streets. Accurate. |
| **Dock Security** | M4 | Dark navy `#2e3d55` | Cyan | Guards the ship. Ren (boss) in black. |
| **HQ Guard** | M5 | Black `#1e2024` | Red | Elite. Sorin (boss) in dark red + gold visor. |
| **Allies** | M2, M5 | Green `#3f6b3a` (Brick) / tan (Lena, Petar) | Green | Follow you after rescue / fight beside you. |

## 4. Missions

### MISSION 0 — CALIBRATION (Practice Range)
- **Location:** Vanguard Range (the original world). **Time:** Midday.
- **Story:** Mara runs Voss through a final calibration before he goes into
  Zlatograd. *"New armour, new rifle, same Vanguard. Show me what you've got."*
- **Gameplay:** Free play. Steel targets, crates, barriers. No enemies.
  Learn to move, aim, reload, crouch, sprint. Tab = showcase orbit.
- **Debrief:** *"Calibration complete. Welcome back, Vanguard. Zlatograd is waiting."*

### MISSION 1 — FIRST LIGHT
- **Location:** **Gornja Luka village** — a small Balkan town at dawn: dirt road,
  plaster houses with pitched roofs, a church tower, market stalls, fences,
  hay bales. **Time:** Dawn, golden fog.
- **Story:** Meridian's militia seized Gornja Luka to plant a Lattice relay.
  Old Petar is hiding the patrol radio codes in the church. Mara needs them —
  without them, she's flying blind for the whole campaign.
- **Objectives:**
  1. Eliminate the village patrol (**6 militia**).
  2. Rescue Old Petar (reach him after the patrol is down — he follows you).
  3. Reach extraction at the village edge.
- **Key dialogue:**
  - Mara: *"Dawn in Gornja Luka. Petar's church, centre of the village. Militia patrol — locals, conscripts. They won't be crack troops, Elias. Still shoot first."*
  - Petar (on rescue): *"You came. After all this time… you came. The codes are in the church book. Take them. And take this town back."*
  - Mara (extraction): *"Codes received. I can hear their chatter now. Elias — we just bought ourselves eyes. Let's use them."*
- **Debrief:** Petar's codes open Meridian's radio net. Mara identifies the
  next target: a relay under construction on the Meridian Tower site.

### MISSION 2 — CONCRETE HEART
- **Location:** **Meridian Tower construction site** — unfinished concrete
  tower, tower crane, scaffolding, container yard, dirt piles, site offices.
  **Time:** Overcast day.
- **Story:** Dr. Lena Aris built the Lattice relays — and stole their master
  codes. She is hiding in the site office while Meridian contractors hunt her.
  The site's three signal jammers are jamming her distress beacon. Kill the
  jammers, get Lena out, and Mara gets the keys to the whole Lattice.
- **Objectives:**
  1. Destroy **3 signal jammers** (shoot the red crates).
  2. Rescue Dr. Lena Aris (she follows you to extraction).
  3. Eliminate site security (**8 contractors**) — they will hunt you.
  4. Reach extraction.
- **Key dialogue:**
  - Lena (radio, static): *"—anyone— the jammers are killing my beacon— please—"*
  - Mara: *"That's Lena. Elias, those jammers are why she's still alive — Meridian can't track her while they're up. Take them down and she can run. Then you run with her."*
  - Lena (rescue): *"The codes. I copied everything. Sorin doesn't know. He thinks I'm still his engineer. Let me show you what he built."*
- **Debrief:** Lena's codes reveal the Lattice kill-switch is on the downtown
  relay — the same relay where Tomas hid his data cache. Mara goes quiet.
  *"That's my brother's relay, Elias. We go downtown next."*

### MISSION 3 — ROOFTOPS
- **Location:** **Downtown Zlatograd** — dense city block: tall apartment
  towers, alley, parked cars, streetlights, shopfronts, rooftop relay.
  **Time:** Dusk, orange sky.
- **Story:** Tomas Kovač hid his data cache on the downtown relay antenna.
  It holds the Lattice kill-switch — and the proof of who ordered his death.
  Meridian Elite now hold the block. Snipers on the rooftops. Patrols in the
  streets. This is the hardest fight yet.
- **Objectives:**
  1. Eliminate the downtown force (**10 elite** — including rooftop snipers).
  2. Reach the rooftop relay cache (reach zone on the relay platform).
  3. Reach extraction.
- **Key dialogue:**
  - Mara: *"Tomas used to climb that relay at night. Said the city looked like a circuit board from up there. …Find his cache, Elias. Bring him home."*
  - Mara (cache found): *"It's him. His handwriting. The kill-switch is real — and there's a name on the kill order. **Commander Ren.** The docks. He did it, Elias. He did it."*
  - Voss: *"Then we go to the docks."*
- **Debrief:** The kill-switch is real. So is Ren's guilt. Mara wants the docks
  tonight. Nadia, a dock smuggler, agrees to guide Voss in.

### MISSION 4 — IRON TIDE
- **Location:** **Zlatograd Port** — container stacks, warehouses, gantry
  cranes, a moored Meridian cargo ship, floodlights, water. **Time:** Night.
- **Story:** The Meridian ship *Iron Tide* is unloading autonomous weapons —
  the live test arsenal. Commander Ren is aboard. Destroy the weapon crates,
  kill Ren, and steal the manifests that prove the test. Nadia guides you
  through the container maze by radio.
- **Objectives:**
  1. Destroy **4 weapons crates** (shoot the marked crates).
  2. Eliminate Commander Ren (**boss, 200 HP**) and dock security (**9**).
  3. Reach extraction at the pier.
- **Key dialogue:**
  - Nadia: *"Crane three is mine. I'll swing the light your way. Stay out of the floodlights, hero — Ren's boys love a lit target."*
  - Mara (Ren spotted): *"That's him. Ren. Elias… Tomas's last message named him. Make it count."*
  - Ren (death): *"Sorin… the test… tell him… tell him the city—"* *(dies)*
  - Mara: *"Manifests secured. The test is in 72 hours. And now we know where Sorin will be watching it from. His tower. We end this at the tower."*
- **Debrief:** With the manifests, Mara can prove everything — if she can
  broadcast them. The Lattice must die first. Brick arrives with a plan:
  a stolen APC, a breached wall, and one last assault on Meridian HQ.

### MISSION 5 — BLACKOUT (Finale)
- **Location:** **Meridian HQ compound** — walled compound, glass HQ tower,
  courtyard, helipad, guard towers. **Time:** Night, storm fog.
- **Story:** Voss and Brick assault Meridian HQ. Brick fights beside you.
  Sorin is in the tower, watching his live test begin. Kill his guard, kill
  Sorin, trigger the kill-switch, and broadcast the truth to the whole city.
- **Objectives:**
  1. Breach the courtyard — eliminate HQ guard (**6 elite**).
  2. Eliminate Viktor Sorin (**boss, 300 HP**).
  3. Reach extraction (helipad).
- **Key dialogue:**
  - Brick: *"APC's mine, kid. I'll park it through the gate. You just make sure the courtyard's empty when I get there. …Try to keep up."*
  - Sorin (radio): *"Captain Voss. I offered you a fortune. I offer you a future. Join Meridian, and Zlatograd is yours to rule."*
  - Voss: *"I already ruled this city once, Viktor. I gave it back."*
  - Sorin (death): *"You… can't… stop… the Lattice…"*
  - Mara (kill-switch triggered): *"Broadcasting now. Every screen in Zlatograd. The manifests, the kill order, Ren's confession. …Elias. Look at the skyline."*
  - *(City lights return. The Lattice dies. Zlatograd wakes up.)*
- **Ending / Epilogue:** Sorin is arrested live on every screen in the city.
  Meridian's contract is torn up in public. Petar rings the church bell in
  Gornja Luka. Lena testifies. Nadia gets her boats back. Brick keeps the APC.
  Mara finally sleeps. And Voss — Vanguard — walks out of Zlatograd at dawn,
  rifle on his back, owing nobody anything.
  > *"Some people protect the city. Some people become it. Elias Voss just
  > wanted to give it back."*

## 5. How to Play

- **WASD** move · **Mouse** aim · **LMB** fire · **RMB** aim down sights
- **Shift** sprint · **C** crouch · **Space** jump · **R** reload · **Q/E** lean
- **Esc** pause / release mouse · **M** mute
- Follow the **objective marker** (diamond + distance). Enemies are tinted
  per faction — olive militia, charcoal contractors, black elite, navy docks.
- Missions unlock in order. Progress is saved in your browser.
- Practice Range (Mission 0) is always open — use it to warm up.
